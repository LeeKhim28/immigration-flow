- Authority: Immigration Department of Malaysia

## Scope checked

The page covers Student Pass categories, higher-education entry conditions, application routing, eVAL/SEV sequencing, post-arrival medical examination, and endorsement.

## V1 findings

- Higher-education applicants include UA and eligible IPTS applicants.
- An IPTS course and institution must have the stated MOHE/MOHA approvals.
- The applicant must be outside Malaysia when a new higher-education application is submitted.
- The application is routed through the institution/STARS or EMGS portal, then forwarded for Immigration eVAL review after academic screening.
- A visa-required applicant obtains SEV only after eVAL approval.
- A new student must complete a post-arrival medical examination before endorsement.

## Limitations and conflicts

- The page says medical examination within seven days; EMGS medical guidance says seven working days. ImmigrationFlow V1 records both statements but uses the stricter seven-calendar-day operational deadline for reminders. An overdue case receives urgent human follow-up and is never automatically rejected.
- Security, travel-document, institution-authorization, and final approval checks are not reproducible by this portfolio system.
- Sabah/Sarawak operational differences are outside V1.

## Rule use

May support scope gates, workflow sequencing, and manual-review flags. Must never be used to represent Immigration approval.
