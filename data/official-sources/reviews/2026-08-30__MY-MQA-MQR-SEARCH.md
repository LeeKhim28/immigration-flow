## V1 findings

The Malaysian Qualifications Register is the official reference point for accredited Malaysian qualifications and higher-education providers. Absence from the full-accreditation search may require checking provisional accreditation or other context.

## Automation boundary

MQR evidence can support programme-accreditation verification but does not, by itself, prove that an IPTS currently has MOHA authorization to enrol international students. V1 therefore records programme and institution verification as human-confirmed inputs rather than scraping or inferring approval.

## Rule use

May support an evidence-backed manual verification task. It is not an automatic eligibility decision.
