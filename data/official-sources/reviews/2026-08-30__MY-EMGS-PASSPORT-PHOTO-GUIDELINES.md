## V1 findings

A submitted photo is expected to be 35 mm wide by 45 mm high, in colour, on a plain white background, recent within six months, professionally taken, unedited, not a selfie, without eyeglasses, evenly lit, front-facing, and limited to the applicant. Religious head coverings are allowed when facial features remain visible.

## Automation boundary

File metadata and user attestations can be checked deterministically. Visual checks may be assisted by AI or a photo-checking service, but results are advisory and cannot claim final acceptance by EMGS or Immigration.

## Rule use

May support photo checklist warnings and machine-checkable age/dimension/file metadata where those inputs are available.
